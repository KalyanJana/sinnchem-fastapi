Company Sinnchem Life Sciences (OPC) Private Limited

Built around the work behind every molecule.

Sinnchem Life Sciences (OPC) Private Limited describes itself as a manufacturer of industrial chemicals and a contract research and manufacturing organization. The company states that it supports R&D programs from lead generation through clinical studies, including work in fine chemicals and APIs.

Its public company profile places client satisfaction, quality, safety, and ethical practices at the center of that work. Product and project requirements can be discussed directly with the team.

Our vision

Service that earns lasting confidence.

Sinnchem states its vision is to achieve high customer satisfaction through exceptional service in custom synthesis and manufacturing.
